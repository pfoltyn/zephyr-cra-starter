## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@EXAMPLE.COM** (PGP key: `<fingerprint>`), or use GitHub's
"Report a vulnerability" (private security advisories) on this repository.

Please include the affected product and firmware version (`app status` in the
device shell prints it), a description, and steps to reproduce.

## What happens next

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 3 working days |
| Initial assessment and severity (CVSS) | 10 working days |
| Fix or mitigation for critical issues | as fast as possible, normally 30 days |
| Public advisory | after a fix is available, coordinated with the reporter |

Actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents are reported to the
authorities as required by the CRA (early warning within 24 hours of becoming
aware). See `docs/cra/README.md` for the internal process.
